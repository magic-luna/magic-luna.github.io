Last updated: September 14, 2026

The application may be used only with a Google account owned or expressly controlled by the application owner. The user is responsible for maintaining the security of the computer, Google account, OAuth credentials, and application configuration.

## Application behavior

LunaGmail reads and processes messages intentionally selected through its designated workflow, manages associated Gmail labels, and sends reminders, summaries, and operational alerts to the same authorized account. The account owner remains responsible for reviewing generated summaries and for decisions or actions based on them.
